# **Week 08 – Intrusion Detection and Risk Management**

## **Overview**

During Week 08, I studied **Intrusion Detection Systems (IDS), Intrusion Prevention Systems (IPS), and cybersecurity risk assessment and treatment**.

## **What I Studied**

- **Network-based IDS (NIDS)**
- **Host-based IDS (HIDS)**
- **IDS and IPS behaviour**
- **Signature-based and anomaly-based detection**
- **True Positives and False Positives**
- **True Negatives and False Negatives**
- **IDS alert tuning**
- **Risk assessment process**
- **Asset, Threat and Vulnerability identification**
- **Qualitative risk analysis**
- **Risk likelihood and consequences**
- **Risk treatment strategies**
- **Avoid, Mitigate, Transfer and Accept**
- **Residual risk**
